Customer Support Specialist

Brightside builds scheduling software for clinics. Our support team answers questions from clinic staff by chat and email, and we are adding two people to the Cebu team.

What you will do

  • Answer customer chats and emails within our response time targets
  • Troubleshoot account and billing issues and escalate bugs to engineering
  • Write and update help centre articles

Requirements

  • At least 1 year of customer support experience, preferably for a software product
  • Excellent written English
  • Comfortable working the night shift
  • Experience with Zendesk is a plus

What we offer

PHP 35,000 to 45,000 per month, HMO, and a fully remote setup within the Philippines.
